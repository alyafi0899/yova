Design a complete modern web platform for creating and publishing beautiful digital wedding invitations, primarily targeted at Muslim weddings in Indonesia.

The product should feel elegant, warm, premium, modern, and easy to use. It should combine the visual beauty of a wedding invitation with the simplicity of a SaaS website builder.

The platform allows users to:

1. Choose a wedding invitation template.
2. Enter and customize wedding information.
3. Upload photos.
4. Customize colors, typography, and visual style.
5. Preview the invitation in real time.
6. Save the invitation as a draft.
7. Publish the invitation after completing payment.
8. Share the published invitation through a unique URL.
9. Manage RSVP responses and wedding wishes.

IMPORTANT PRODUCT MODEL:
Users can create and fully preview their invitation for free. Payment is only required when they want to publish the invitation publicly.

Design the product as a responsive web application with a strong mobile-first mindset because the final wedding invitations will primarily be viewed on smartphones.
