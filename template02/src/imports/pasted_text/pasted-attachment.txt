# Create One Premium Muslim Wedding E-Invitation Template: “SAKINAH”

Design and prototype **ONE complete Muslim wedding e-invitation template** called **SAKINAH**.

Do NOT create:

* Landing page
* Template marketplace
* Template selection page
* Pricing comparison
* Marketing website

The focus is entirely on the **actual wedding invitation experience and its supporting management interfaces**.

The design should be production-ready and suitable to later implement as a real web application.

---

# 1. TEMPLATE CONCEPT

## SAKINAH

A premium modern Muslim wedding invitation inspired by:

* Islamic elegance
* Modern editorial wedding design
* Romantic luxury
* Warm intimate atmosphere
* Minimal Islamic ornamentation
* Premium Indonesian wedding aesthetic

The template should feel sophisticated and expensive without becoming overly decorative.

Avoid:

* Excessive gold
* Excessive Arabic ornaments
* Generic wedding-template aesthetics
* Cartoon-like elements
* Overly complicated layouts
* Cheap gradients
* Excessive animations
